# 2026-05-21｜Wrong Order at the Café

> 教材：MJ English｜[Wrong Order Café English](https://mjenglishclass.com/daily-english/wrong-order-cafe-english)

## 本集大意

Amy 點了焦糖拿鐵卻拿到一般拿鐵，向店員有禮貌且清楚地確認並更換，同時確認帳單沒有多收錢。MJ 解析 Amy 如何透過 `I think`、`mix-up` 等說法表達訴求，而不是在指責對方。

---

## 一、MJ English 本集重點

### 核心句型

| 句型 | 說明 | 範例 |
|------|------|------|
| `I think there's been a mix-up with my ___` | 用 `I think` 軟化語氣，不直接指責 | I think there's been a mix-up with my order. |
| `I ordered ___, but I got ___ instead` | 清楚說明點什麼、拿到什麼 | I ordered a caramel latte, but I got a regular latte instead. |
| `Could you swap this out for ___?` | 禮貌要求更換 | Could you swap this out for the right one? |
| `Could you make sure I'm not charged for ___?` | 確認帳單沒有多收錢 | Could you make sure I'm not charged for the wrong item? |
| `No worries, it happens!` | 大方收尾，不讓對方難堪 | No worries, it happens! |

### 關鍵片語

| 片語 | 意思 | 範例 |
|------|------|------|
| `mix-up` | 搞錯、弄混（不帶指責） | There's been a mix-up with the orders. |
| `swap out` | 換掉（比 replace / change 更口語） | Could you swap this out for the right one? |
| `sort out` | 處理好、解決 | Let me sort this out for you right away. |
| `double-check` | 再確認一次 | Could you double-check my receipt? |
| `right away` | 馬上、立刻 | I'll fix that right away. |
| `charged for` | 被收費 | Make sure I'm not charged for the wrong item. |

## 二、延伸學習

你貼出的句子，以及從中延伸分析出的句型。

**原句：** She's keeping an eye on the bill without making it a big deal.

### `keep an eye on ___`

**意思：** 持續留意某個對象（主動監控，不是一次性查看）

**IT 場景：** The on-call engineer is keeping an eye on the CPU usage.

### `___ without making it a big deal`

**意思：** 低調處理某件事，不小題大做

**IT 場景：** He flagged the bug without making it a big deal.

### `keep an ear out for ___`（延伸）

**意思：** 等待聽到某個特定消息（被動等候，不確定何時出現）

| 片語 | 感覺 |
|------|------|
| `keep an eye on ___` | 持續盯著某個對象（主動監控） |
| `keep an ear out for ___` | 等待聽到某個消息（被動等候） |

> `keep an eye on` 是固定片語，不能把 `eye` 換成 `ear`，兩者語意也不同。

**「偷偷在聽」要說：**
- **eavesdrop on** someone（最常用）
- **listen in on** a conversation（中性一點）
